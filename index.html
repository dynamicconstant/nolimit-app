      About the analysis

      Each decision is compared with the best estimated option at that moment: fold, check or call, and a few raise sizes. The estimates use the bot's equity tables and a simple model of how often opponents fold. They flag clear mistakes well and should not be read as solver-exact. Hindsight lines use cards you could not see and never change the score.
